How to play

Sneak goods off the shelves while the bulldog boss isn't looking. Each round lasts 40 seconds.

  • Tap goods on the side he isn't watching: +1. Tap the side he's watching and you're caught: −2.
  • Watch his face: his ears prick up and he glares just before he turns. Sometimes it's a bluff.
  • Grab different goods in a row for a combo: each side item is worth 1 more than the last, each middle item 2 more.
  • Once a round he naps: grab the middle shelf behind him before he wakes up.
  • Every round uses one shopping cart. Out of carts? Wait for free ones or watch a short video.
